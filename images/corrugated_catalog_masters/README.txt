Corrugated Packaging Catalogue Image Masters

Source handling
- Only the existing high-resolution generated PNGs were used.
- No 172 px references were upscaled.
- Native square images were preserved at their native dimensions.
- Non-square sources were centered and padded to a square canvas; no pixel enlargement was performed.

Outputs
- masters/: lossless PNG archive masters
- web/: AVIF derivatives
- webp/: WebP derivatives
- manifest.csv / manifest.json: source-to-output mapping

Important
These exports preserve the source imagery as generated. They do not remove printed text/branding or alter product construction.
